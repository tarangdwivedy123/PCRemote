# PC Remote

Control your Windows PC from your phone — volume, music, monitor inputs,
brightness and power — over your own Wi-Fi.

**[Download it here](https://tarangdwivedy123.github.io/PCRemote/)**

No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your network. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
